## 👨‍💻 About Me

I’m a **Computer Science student** who enjoys solving complex problems, especially algorithmic ones. When I build something, I like to compare different approaches and look for a solution that is both **effective** and **practical**. I’m also deeply interested in **AI and Machine Learning**, and enjoy putting what I learn into real projects rather than leaving it as theory.

- 🎓 Studying **Computer Science** at **CTUET** (Can Tho University of Technology).
- 🧠 Interested in **Algorithms, Data Structures**, and **Machine Learning**.
- 🔬 Exploring **Computer Vision**, **Vietnamese NLP**, and **Browser-based AI Inference**.
- 🛠️ Enjoy experimenting with different model architectures and optimizing performance.
- 🌱 Always learning, building, and improving.
